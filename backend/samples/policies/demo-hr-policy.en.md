# Demo Trading Ltd — Employee Handbook (HR policies)

Version 2026.1, effective 1 January 2026. ILLUSTRATIVE demo policy for testing the AI assistant; not legal advice.

## Office hours

Office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a lunch and prayer break from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM. The weekly holidays are Friday and Saturday at Dhaka HQ and Friday at the Chattogram office. During Ramadan, office hours are 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM.

## Attendance and late arrival

Every employee must punch in and out on the biometric device or the My Office app inside the office area. Arriving more than 15 minutes after the start time counts as late. Three late arrivals in one month are counted as one day of casual leave; if no casual leave is left, one day of salary is deducted. A missing punch must be corrected through an attendance correction request within 3 working days.

## Casual leave

Every permanent employee gets 10 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and cannot be encashed. Casual leave must be applied for in the My Office app at least one day in advance, except in an emergency. No more than 3 days of casual leave can be taken at a time.

## Sick leave

Employees get 14 days of sick leave per year on full pay. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate from a registered doctor, uploaded within 3 days of returning to work. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Earned leave

Earned (annual) leave accrues at one day for every 18 days worked. Unused earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 40 days; days above 40 are encashed at the basic salary rate at the end of the year. Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.

## Maternity leave

Female employees with at least six months of service get 16 weeks (112 days) of maternity leave on full pay: 8 weeks before and 8 weeks after the expected date of delivery. Maternity benefit is available for the first two children.

## Public holidays

The company follows the government's public holiday calendar, including the Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha holidays. Employees who work on a public holiday get a substitute day off within 30 days.

## Salary payment

Salary is paid by the 7th working day of the following month, to the employee's approved bank account or mobile wallet (bKash or Nagad). The payslip is available in the My Office app and is also sent by e-mail as a password-protected PDF. To change the salary account, submit a bank account change request in the app; HR must approve it before the next payroll.

## Festival bonus

Employees confirmed in service get two festival bonuses per year, before Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha. Each bonus equals one month's basic salary. Employees on probation get a bonus proportional to the months worked.

## Provident fund

After confirmation, employees contribute 10% of basic salary to the provident fund and the company contributes the same amount. The company's contribution is fully vested after 5 years of service.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by the department head. Overtime is paid at twice the basic hourly rate. Total overtime cannot exceed 20 hours in a month.

## Resignation and notice period

A permanent employee who resigns must give 60 days' written notice. If the notice period is not served, basic salary for the shortfall is deducted from the final settlement. The final settlement is paid within 30 working days after the last working day.

## Code of conduct

Employees must treat colleagues with respect. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated and can be reported to HR in confidence. Office equipment and data must be used for company work only.
